The Trip

125 miles. 6 days. Iceland's south coast.

Not a race — no chip timing, no podium, no cutoff clock. It's a supported stage crossing with about 20 runners covering volcanic desert, black beaches, canyons and waterfalls together — some running every mile, some hiking the hard sections. Trip is run through Alive; Justin's just one of the crew on it.
